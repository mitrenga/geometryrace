# geometryrace

Remake of **Geometry Race** (Crimson Pine Games, Android 2015–2020: an
endless runner in 3D — a ship flies towards the horizon, the player steers
left and right through geometric obstacles, energy runs out unless
collected) for the browser, on the **svision** library and its **3D
platform** (R-032, `../DESIGN-3d.md`: a DOM-free scene, a WebGL2 renderer
with a canvas 2D overlay for the HUD and dialogs).

State: **the worlds, the ships, the store and the saved progress (GR-M8,
2026-09-16)** over the pickups (GR-M7), the track and the collisions
(GR-M6), the core loop (GR-M5: the ship, the steering, the camera, the
energy after the notes) and the data (GR-M4) on top of the application
skeleton (GR-M3): boot, menu with the library's dialogs over the 3D
picture — and the game's own: the ship store, the worlds, the hall of
fame — the game screen with a worker at the original's fixed step (50 Hz)
and seeded randomness, rows of blocks generated ahead as the original
does, obstacles switched on and animated, pickups, a crash on contact,
HUD with the record, a game over that books the run into the progress
(localStorage, versioned) and the hall of fame; fingerprints under Node;
the match tools and the instrumented original wait for the operator's
traces from the emulator. The own
content is in `data/` — eight ships (`mkships`), three worlds, an own
track of 32 blocks generated by rules (`mksections`), the numbers of the
mechanics in `global.json` — and checked by `checkdata` (374 checks); the
reference of the original (`unity2params`, `unity2sections`) lives in
`sources/extract/json/`, outside git, for the match tools. GR-M0 (sources)
is downloaded and hash-verified, signature and antivirus checked; the
emulator run is the operator's. GR-M1 (the facts of the original,
`sources/docs/decompile_notes.md`) and GR-M2 (the 3D platform in svision)
are done. Next: the traces (GR-M5/M6 acceptance), then GR-M7 (the pickups).
Specification: `ZADANI.md`. Milestones: `MILNIKY.md` (GR-M0..M9).
Sources plan and rules: `sources/README.md` (the original is proprietary;
nothing of it is versioned or published). Next: the traces (GR-M5–M8
acceptance), then GR-M9 (sound, controls, PWA).

The mechanics are to be behaviour-exact against traces of the running
original; the look, sound and texts are new. Public name: GEOMETRY RACE
(the operator's decision; the project is a study and is not published).

## Running

Like every project here: `index.php` through `app/svision/php/main.php`,
`config/config.php` with `$devMode = true`, `svtool build` for a deploy.
Keys: arrows or A/D steer, space or Escape pause (Escape in the pause quits),
N mutes the sounds, M the music. Touch: the left and right halves of the screen steer.
Without nginx: `php -S 127.0.0.1:8791 router.php` with a router that rewrites
a missing path to `index.php?<path>` (the `nginx.conf` rule of the other projects).

## Developer parameters

In dev mode only (`$devMode` in `config/config.php`; R-058, `Tool.devParam`):
`?devSeed=N` pins the seed of the game's randomness so a run can be
repeated. The same knob goes into the configuration as
`$devMode = ['seed' => 123]`; the address wins. On a production server the
parameter does not exist.
